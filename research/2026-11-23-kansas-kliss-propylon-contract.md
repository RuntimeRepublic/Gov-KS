# Kansas KLISS Modernization Project and Propylon contract

Research note | November 23, 2026

The Kansas Legislature and Propylon, Inc. executed the KLISS Modernization Project contract on October 19, 2022. The signed agreement, identified in the supplied research as the **Computer Software Implementation Contract**, and any signed addenda were not located in the public sources reviewed for this note. The Legislative Coordinating Council (LCC) and committee records summarize the procurement and project milestones. The disclosure status of the contract itself remains to be established through the records custodian.

## Verified in official records

- On September 23, 2022, the LCC approved the contract terms and authorized its chair to sign. Alan Weis, the Legislature's Chief Information Technology Officer, reported that the LCC chair and Propylon signed on October 19, 2022. January 2026 testimony also gives that execution date. [November 16, 2022 JCIT report](https://www.kslegislature.gov/li_2022/b2021_22/committees/misc/20221116alanweislegcitoquarrpts.pdf) · [January 14, 2026 legislative modernization testimony](https://kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/committees/testimony/pdf/?apn=b2025_26%2Fyear2%2Fhouse%2Fcommittees%2Fctte_h_legislative_modernization_1%2Ftestimony%2Fpublished%2Fctte_h_legislative_modernization_1_20260114_01_testimony.html)
- The project steering committee reported that the June 30, 2025 deployment did not proceed because required features were unavailable, including conference committee report, appropriations, and additional table functionality. The committee then targeted a session-ready system before October 1, 2025. [July 7, 2025 steering committee report](https://ksrevisor.gov.ksrevisor.org/docs/LCC_Packet20250707/Item_5_KLISS_Modernization.pdf)

## Details from the supplied research to verify against primary records

- **Cost.** The original contract amount is reported as $3,951,210. Later project spending is reported at approximately $8 million with extensions and additional work. Confirm the original award, amounts authorized under amendments, and amounts actually paid.
- **Scope.** Improve bill and amendment drafting, convert the document editor to Microsoft Word, update Revisor and chamber systems, and update the legislative website while preserving existing functionality.
- **Governance.** The contract reportedly tied payment or performance to deliverables, gave division directors acceptance authority, and assigned oversight to the project steering committee. Check the signed agreement for the exact acceptance and payment terms.
- **Phasing.** Phase 1 reportedly covered bill and resolution drafting, amendments, workflow, codification, Word conversion, templates, and chamber integration, with an early target around October 2024 and a later mid-2025 go-live target. Phase 2 reportedly covered remaining chamber, committee, legislative research, website, and appropriations systems.
- **Later actions.** The research identifies stop-work orders in late 2025 and April 2026 authorization to negotiate a Phase 1 addendum targeting January 2027 with tighter budget, schedule, acceptance, and governance terms. Confirm the orders, the authorization, and whether an addendum was executed.
- **Records to locate.** Alan Weis's July 31, 2023 JCIT report and later LCC packets and steering committee updates are leads for confirming these details.

## Kansas Open Records Act request

Request electronic copies of the executed Computer Software Implementation Contract for the KLISS Modernization Project with Propylon, Inc., signed on or about October 19, 2022, and all subsequent addenda, amendments, stop-work orders, and related agreements. Include your name and contact information. Possible starting points are Legislative Administrative Services for the LCC, the Office of the Revisor of Statutes, and the Kansas Legislative Office of Information Services.

Under K.S.A. 45-218, the agency must act on the request by the end of the third business day after receipt. If access takes longer, the custodian must explain the delay. Fees may apply. If the request is denied, you can ask for a written citation to the legal grounds. [K.S.A. 45-218](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch45/045_002_0018.html)
